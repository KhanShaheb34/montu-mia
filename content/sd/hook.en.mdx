Montu's nagging doubts had finally vanished. The truth was a bit of a bitter pill to swallow, but he accepted that he couldn't just have everything he wanted.

Afternoon had faded into night. Montu got out of his chair, ready to head home, when Boltu Bhai casually tossed out a simple question, "By the way, Montu, what kind of databases are you using for BiralTube? SQL or NoSQL? Or something else entirely?"

Montu's eyes went wide. "Wait, what's all that, Bhai? I just set up everything using a single MySQL database! Do databases have different breeds or something?"

Boltu Bhai burst out laughing. "To find that out, you'll have to come back another day. For now, go home and get some sleep!"
